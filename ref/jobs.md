## Job 6: Senior Laravel Developer

We are looking for a Senior Laravel Developer with at least 5 years of experience. In this role, you will be responsible for building and maintaining robust, scalable, and high-performance web applications using the Laravel framework. The ideal candidate will have experience with the full software development lifecycle, from concept and design to testing and deployment.

### Key Responsibilities:
- Lead the design and development of scalable web applications using PHP (Laravel Framework).
- Architect robust, secure, and high-performance backend systems to support complex business requirements.
- Manage and mentor a team of developers; conduct code reviews and provide technical guidance.
- Collaborate with product managers, designers, and stakeholders to define technical requirements.
- Optimize application performance, troubleshoot complex issues, and implement improvements as needed.
- Oversee project timelines, sprint planning, and task allocation to ensure on-time delivery.
- Implement RESTful APIs and integrate third-party services to enhance the functionality of applications.
- Maintain comprehensive documentation for code, processes, and systems to ensure clarity and consistency.
- Write clean, production-grade Laravel code daily, ensuring the highest coding standards.
- Take ownership of end-to-end feature delivery — from requirement discussion to deployment in production.
- Design and maintain scalable APIs and backend services to support front-end and mobile integrations.
- Handle code reviews, debugging, and resolution of production issues in a timely manner.
- Guide junior/mid developers by not just assigning tasks, but helping improve their problem-solving and coding skills.
- Work closely with stakeholders to translate business problems into effective technical solutions.
- Improve existing systems by refactoring code, optimizing performance, and reducing technical debt.
- Ensure proper security, logging, and monitoring practices are adhered to throughout development.

### Qualifications & Skills:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent work experience).
- 5+ years of professional experience in backend development using PHP, particularly with the Laravel framework.
- Strong knowledge of web development technologies, including MySQL, JavaScript, and RESTful APIs.
- Proficient in version control systems, especially Git.
- Proven experience in leading and mentoring development teams.
- Strong problem-solving skills and ability to troubleshoot complex technical issues.
- Ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams to define technical requirements.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, both verbal and written.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and sprint planning.
- Experience with testing frameworks (PHPUnit, etc.) and ensuring code quality.

### Desirable Skills:
- Experience with containerization tools like Docker.
- Knowledge of front-end technologies (Vue.js, React) is a plus.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, etc.) for deployment and scalability.
